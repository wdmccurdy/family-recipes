# Okonomiyaki Savory Cabbage Pancakes – Japanese Night

**Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 20 min | **Servings:** 4  
**Dietary notes:** Vegetarian option, kid-customizable “pizza-pancakes,” gluten-free adaptable

![Featured Image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1627308595229-92d4f9e2c8b0)

## Ingredients
- 6 packed cups finely shredded cabbage (about 1 medium head)
- 2½ cups chopped scallions (about 2 bunches)
- 2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- 1½ tsp sea salt
- 6 large eggs, beaten
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for brushing skillet

**For serving (the fun part!):**
- Worcestershire sauce (vegan or regular)
- Mayo (Kewpie or regular — squeeze bottle for stripes!)
- Sesame seeds
- Pickled ginger
- 1 sheet nori, sliced into thin strips
- Microgreens or extra scallions (optional)

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine cabbage, scallions, panko, and salt. Gently mix in the beaten eggs. The mixture will be very loose and cabbage-heavy — that’s exactly right! Let it sit 5-10 min if it looks too dry.
2. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Brush generously with olive oil.
3. Scoop ¼ of the mixture (use a ½-cup measure) into the skillet. Gently flatten with a spatula to about ½-inch thick. Cook 3-4 minutes until golden brown, then flip and cook the other side another 3 minutes (lower heat if browning too fast).
4. Repeat with remaining batter, adding more oil as needed. Wipe skillet between batches.
5. Drizzle each pancake generously with Worcestershire sauce and thin stripes of mayo. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, pickled ginger, nori strips, and microgreens. Serve immediately while hot and crispy!

## Tips & Tricks
- Shred cabbage very finely (mandoline or food processor works best) so pancakes hold together.  
- Make-ahead: batter can sit 30 min in fridge. Cook fresh for best texture.

## Fun Family Twist
Turn it into a build-your-own Japanese pancake pizza party — kids pick and pile on their favorite toppings!
