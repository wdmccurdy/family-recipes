## Starter: Eggplant Dip with Yogurt & Bread

*Recipe coming soon.*

**Most likely what we had:** **Zaalouk** — Moroccan cooked salad of eggplant, tomato, garlic, and olive oil (reddish, jammy dip). Often served at room temperature with:

- Plain yogurt or **lebneh**-style yogurt on the side or dolloped on top
- Warm **pita** or **naan** for scooping

**Also possible:** A tomato–red pepper salad (**matbucha** / **salade cuite**) alongside eggplant, or **harissa** stirred into the red sauce for heat.

**Notes for later:** Was the eggplant mostly mashed or chunky? Any cumin, paprika, or cilantro on top?

## Main: Chicken with Warm Spices & Apricots

**Most likely what we had:** A **chicken tagine** — bone-in chicken pieces braised with warm spices (cinnamon, ginger, turmeric, cumin, coriander), onion, and **dried apricots** (sweet-savory Moroccan profile). Often includes olives or almonds; served with couscous or bread if you had it.
