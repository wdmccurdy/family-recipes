# 🇵🇪 Peru Family Dinner Night  
**Citrusy & Fresh – Ceviche + Lomo Saltado**  

This is a vibrant South American adventure with Japanese-Peruvian fusion flair — bright, zesty, and completely new to your rotation. Ceviche “cooks” in lime (no stove for the starter!), and lomo saltado is a quick, saucy stir-fry that tosses fries right into the pan. Fresh, colorful, shareable, and feels like a mini coastal vacation.  
**Total hands-on time:** ~45–60 minutes (serves 4, easy to scale). Family-friendly & mild by default.

## Full Menu
- **Starter:** Classic Peruvian Ceviche (light, refreshing “no-cook” crowd-pleaser)  
- **Main:** Lomo Saltado (tender beef stir-fry with fries, tomatoes, onions & sauce)  
- **Side:** Fluffy white rice (soaks up all the juices)  
- **Dessert:** Fresh mango slices or passionfruit (simple & tropical)

## 🛒 Shopping List (Sunnyvale easy)
- 1.5 lbs very fresh white fish (sea bass, cod, halibut, tilapia OR shrimp)  
- 1 lb beef (flank steak, sirloin, or tenderloin)  
- 8–10 limes (Key limes best)  
- 2 red onions  
- 3 ripe tomatoes  
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro  
- 1 sweet potato + 1 ear corn (or frozen kernels)  
- 1 lb frozen French fries  
- Garlic (5–6 cloves total)  
- Soy sauce, vegetable/canola oil, salt & pepper  
- 1.5–2 cups rice (jasmine or long-grain)  
- Mangoes or passionfruit for dessert  
**Optional but authentic:** small jar aji amarillo paste (World Market / H-Mart / Asian aisle)

## 🔥 Recipes

### Starter: Classic Peruvian Ceviche
**Ingredients**  
- 1.5 lbs fresh white fish (cut into ½-inch cubes)  
- 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced (reserve some for garnish)  
- 1 cup fresh lime juice (8–10 limes)  
- 2 garlic cloves, minced  
- ½ bunch cilantro, chopped  
- Salt & black pepper  
- To serve: boiled sweet potato slices + corn kernels  

**Steps**  
1. Place fish cubes in a glass bowl. Add lime juice, garlic, most of the sliced onion, salt, and pepper. Gently mix.  
2. Cover and refrigerate 15–30 minutes until fish turns opaque white.  
3. Stir in chopped cilantro. Taste and adjust seasoning.  
4. Serve cold with sweet potato slices, corn, and extra onion rings on top.

### Main: Lomo Saltado (Peruvian Beef Stir-Fry)
**Ingredients**  
- 1 lb beef, thinly sliced against the grain  
- 3–4 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar (or extra lime juice)  
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1 large red onion, cut into thick wedges  
- 2–3 tomatoes, cut into wedges  
- 1 lb French fries (baked or air-fried until crisp)  
- 2–3 tbsp vegetable oil  
- Salt, pepper, and chopped cilantro/scallions for garnish  
- Optional: 1 tsp aji amarillo paste or pinch of cumin  

**Steps**  
1. Marinate beef in 2 tbsp soy sauce, garlic, vinegar, salt & pepper for 10–15 min.  
2. Cook fries according to package.  
3. Heat large wok or skillet on high with 1–2 tbsp oil. Sear beef quickly (1–2 min per side). Remove and set aside.  
4. In same pan, stir-fry onion and tomato wedges 1–2 min (keep them slightly firm).  
5. Return beef to pan, add remaining soy sauce (and optional aji), then gently toss in hot fries. Cook 30–60 seconds more.  
6. Garnish with cilantro/scallions and serve immediately over rice.

### Side: Fluffy White Rice
Cook 1.5–2 cups rice per your usual method (rice cooker works great). Fluff and keep warm.

### Dessert
Slice fresh mango or scoop passionfruit. Add a squeeze of lime if desired. Serve chilled.

## ✅ Quick Tips
- Prep ceviche first so it marinates while you cook the main.  
- Keep it mild — serve extra lime or a tiny dish of aji on the side.  
- Kids love squeezing limes or tossing the fries in at the end.  
- Leftovers are excellent cold the next day.  
- Veggie swap: use tofu or extra veggies instead of beef/fish.

## 🎉 Vibe
Bright colorful plates, upbeat Peruvian music (or a quick YouTube video about Lima’s food scene), and let everyone build their own plates. It’s fresh, fun, and feels like a real family adventure!
