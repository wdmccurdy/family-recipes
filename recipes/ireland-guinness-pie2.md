# Guinness Beef Pie & Chocolate Guinness Cake – Irish Night

**Prep Time:** 40 min | **Cook Time:** 3 hrs (pie) + 45 min (cake) | **Servings:** 4  
**Dietary notes:** Kid-friendly (mild flavors), non-alcoholic Guinness option, make-ahead pie filling

![Featured Image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1608039755401-742074f0548d)

## Ingredients
### Guinness Beef Pie
- 1.5 lbs beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 bottle (11.2 oz) Guinness stout (or non-alcoholic version)
- 2 medium onions, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 sheet frozen puff pastry, thawed
- 1 egg + 1 tbsp milk (for egg wash)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil

### Guinness Chocolate Cake with Baileys Creme Frosting
- 1 cup Guinness stout
- 1 cup unsalted butter
- ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- ¾ cup sour cream
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1½ tsp baking soda
- **Frosting:** 8 oz cream cheese (softened), 1 cup powdered sugar, ⅓ cup Baileys Irish Cream, 1 tsp vanilla

## Instructions
1. **Make the pie filling:** Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high. Season beef with salt and pepper, brown in batches (5-6 min). Remove and set aside.
2. Add onions, carrots, celery, and mushrooms to the pot. Cook 8-10 min until softened. Stir in flour and tomato paste, cook 2 min.
3. Pour in Guinness, scraping up browned bits. Add broth, Worcestershire, thyme, and browned beef. Bring to boil, then simmer covered on low for 2 hours until beef is fork-tender. Cool slightly.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F. Transfer filling to a 9-inch pie dish. Top with puff pastry, crimp edges, cut vents, and brush with egg wash. Bake 25-30 min until golden and bubbling.
5. **Make the cake:** Preheat oven to 350°F. In a saucepan, simmer Guinness and butter. Whisk in cocoa and sugar until smooth. Cool 10 min.
6. Beat in eggs one at a time, then sour cream and vanilla. Whisk in flour and baking soda. Pour into greased 9-inch pan and bake 45 min. Cool completely.
7. Beat frosting ingredients until fluffy. Spread on cooled cake. Optional: drizzle extra Baileys on top!

## Tips & Tricks
- Pie filling can be made 2 days ahead and refrigerated.  
- For kids: use non-alcoholic Guinness and skip Baileys in frosting (use milk instead).
