Starter: Spanish Cheese Board

Typical spread for a Spanish-themed night — mix and match what you had:

  • Manchego (or another firm Spanish cheese), sliced
  • Membrillo (quince paste) with cheese
  • Marcona almonds or mixed nuts
  • Green olives and/or gordal olives
  • Sliced chorizo or jamón
  • Good bread or crackers
  • Optional: pan con tomate (rubbed tomato on toasted bread with olive oil and salt)

Main: Tortilla Española (Spanish Potato Omelet)

Recipe coming soon.

The classic thick “pancake” of sliced potatoes and onion bound with eggs — cooked slowly in olive oil, often flipped once for a set center. Served warm or at room temperature, in wedges.

Likely what we made: Olive oil, thin-sliced potato and onion, beaten eggs, salt; low-and-slow in a skillet (often 8–10 inch) until tender, then set the egg.

Notes for later: Did you use a flip or finish under the broiler? Onion cooked in with potatoes or separate?
