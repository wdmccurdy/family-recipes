Brazil Theme Night Menu (Cheese Bread + Mild Moqueca Stew + Brigadeiros)

Theme ideas: Green/yellow/blue plates or napkins (Brazil flag colors). Play upbeat Brazilian music. Use lime wedges and cilantro for pretty pops of color.

1. Starter: Pão de Queijo (Brazilian Cheese Bread)

Chewy, cheesy gluten-free puffs. Toddlers love them as finger food; tear/mash for infant. Blender method = super easy. Makes ~12–18 rolls.

Ingredients:

  • 1 large egg (room temp)
  • ⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil (or neutral oil)
  • ⅔ cup milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1½ cups (170g) tapioca flour (or tapioca starch — find in baking or gluten-free aisle)
  • ½ cup (66g) grated cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or Parmesan — whatever you like)
  • Pinch of salt (optional)
  • Extra oil or cooking spray for greasing muffin tin

Quick Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Grease a mini muffin tin.
  2. Add all ingredients to a blender. Blend until smooth (batter will be thin).
  3. Pour into muffin cups, filling ¾ full.
  4. Bake 15–20 minutes until puffed and golden. Cool slightly (they deflate a bit — that's normal). Serve warm.

Kid tip: Make a double batch — they disappear fast and reheat well.

2. Main: Mild Moqueca (Brazilian Coconut Stew — Chicken Version)

Creamy, colorful one-pot stew with coconut milk, veggies, and lime. Super soft and scoopable over rice. Use chicken for zero seafood worry (or swap in tilapia if you prefer). Mild and fragrant — adventurous but approachable.

Ingredients (serves 4):

  • 1.5–2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs, cut into bite-size chunks (or 1.5–2 lbs firm white fish like tilapia)
  • 2–3 limes (juice for marinating + serving)
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 2 bell peppers (1 red + 1 yellow for sweetness and color), sliced
  • 3–4 ripe tomatoes, chopped (or 1 can diced tomatoes)
  • 2 cans (13.5 oz each) full-fat coconut milk
  • Small bunch fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • 2–3 Tbsp olive oil or neutral oil
  • Salt & black pepper to taste
  • Optional mild boost: 1 tsp paprika (not spicy)
  • For serving: White rice (1–2 cups dry) + optional canned black beans

Quick Instructions:

  1. Marinate protein: Toss chicken (or fish) with juice of 1–2 limes, garlic, salt & pepper. Let sit 10–15 min while you chop veggies.
  2. In a large pot, heat oil over medium. Sauté onion and bell peppers 5 min until softened. Add tomatoes and cook 3–4 min.
  3. Add marinated chicken (or fish) and any marinade. Pour in coconut milk. Bring to gentle simmer.
  4. Simmer 15–20 min (chicken) or 8–10 min (fish) until protein is cooked through and sauce thickens slightly. Stir gently.
  5. Finish with juice of remaining lime and most of the cilantro. Taste and add salt if needed.
  6. Serve over rice with extra lime wedges and cilantro on top. Mash or cut small for little ones.

Kid tip: The coconut milk makes it silky and slightly sweet. Blend a portion if your infant prefers puree.

3. Dessert: Brigadeiros (No-Bake Chocolate Truffles)

Fudgy chocolate balls rolled in sprinkles. Toddlers can help roll them (messy fun!). Makes ~15–20.

Ingredients:

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 2–3 Tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 Tbsp butter (plus extra for greasing hands)
  • Chocolate sprinkles (for rolling — lots!)

Quick Instructions:

  1. In a medium saucepan over medium-low heat, add condensed milk, cocoa powder, and butter.
  2. Stir constantly (important!) for 8–12 minutes until mixture thickens to a fudge-like consistency — it should pull away from the sides of the pan.
  3. Pour into a greased plate or bowl. Let cool to room temp, then refrigerate 1–2 hours until firm.
  4. Grease hands with butter, scoop small spoonfuls, and roll into balls. Roll in sprinkles.
  5. Place in mini paper cups (optional, for cute presentation). Chill until serving.

Kid tip: Let your toddler help with the rolling step (with supervision). They store well in the fridge.
